### 🩺 MediAssist
**Multimodal Medical Report Assistant**

> *Making medical reports understandable for everyone.*

**The problem:** Lab and medical reports are full of jargon that confuses patients.

**The approach:** Let users upload a report, get plain-language explanations, and ask follow-up questions conversationally.

**What it explores**
- 📄 Report upload & understanding
- 🗣️ Medical terms in simple language
- 💬 Conversational Q&A over report content
- ♿ Accessible, clean UX
